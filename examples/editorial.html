The pause between places

We often describe a city by its destinations. The station, the office, the place where we meet a friend. But much of our experience takes shape in the intervals: a staircase, a shaded threshold, the turn into a quieter street.

Look closely and the rigid outline begins to soften. Concrete holds the trace of a surface. Repeated windows make a rhythm. A corner that seemed severe from a distance becomes a place to stop and watch the light change.

This is a small invitation to notice what is already there. No new route is required. Only a little more attention to the one we take each day.

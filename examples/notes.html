Notes from a slow Saturday

I started the morning at the record shop on Ferry Street, where a turntable had been left spinning in the window long after the song ended. Nobody seemed in a hurry to lift the needle.

The station escalators were running again after a month of repairs. I rode them twice, once for the view and once because I had missed my stop.

Some places are only interesting when you are not trying to get anywhere.

On the walk home I passed the old library, its concrete beams sharp against a flat gray sky, then spent the rest of the afternoon in my favorite chair with a book I have been not-quite-reading since August.

Tomorrow, the same plan. Fewer stops, if I can manage it.
